Each Milestone consists of two parts: **Documentation** and **Technology**. Milestone 2 documents your transition from design to implementation, providing concrete evidence of technical progress and system calibration.

---

## 1. Documentation

This report must provide a rigorous bridge between your theoretical models and the actual data flowing through your ROS 2 nodes.

### Required Sections

1. **Instructor Feedback Integration:** A table listing every critique received from Milestone 1 and the specific technical actions taken to adjust your approach or architecture.
2. **Mathematical Kinematics:** A formal description of your robot's motion model using **LaTeX** equations. This must explicitly show the mapping from control inputs (e.g., wheel velocities) to state updates (e.g., $x, y, \theta$).
3. **Detailed Topic Map:** An updated Mermaid diagram or a high-resolution `rqt_graph` export. Since the project is still in progress, you must clearly distinguish between completed and pending work:
   1. **Completed Modules**: Every connection must be labeled with **Topic Names** and **Message Types** (e.g., `/cmd_vel` [geometry_msgs/msg/Twist] or `/scan` [sensor_msgs/msg/LaserScan]).
   2. **Pending Modules**: Identify which modules from your Milestone 1 architecture are not yet implemented.
4. **Noise & Uncertainty Analysis:**
* **Hardware:** Report on sensor calibration (offsets), measured variance/noise profiles during tests, or parameter tuning to adapt to noise.
* **Simulation:** Provide the logic and code snippet for your **Noise Injector** node. You must demonstrate how your custom algorithms perform under synthetic sensor noise.

1. **Robustness Audit:** Document any system behaviors during your runs/tests such as recovery logic after a simulated LiDAR timeout, temporary loss of odometry, or unexpected obstacles.
2. **Milestone Video:** Link to one or more embedded YouTube/Vimeo video showing the robot executing a core sub-task (e.g., autonomous mapping, goal-to-goal navigation, or sensor fusion stability).

---

## 2. Technology

The technical portion of this milestone (40% of the MS2 grade) focuses on the stability of your communication stack and your ability to simulate real-world conditions.

* **Communication Proof:** All nodes defined in your Topic Map must be functional and communicating with correct message types.
* **Math-to-Code Alignment:** Your ROS 2 nodes must accurately reflect the kinematic equations defined in your LaTeX section.
* **The Noise Injector:** For simulation teams, the noise injector must be a standalone node capable of modifying clean simulation data to mimic real-world uncertainty.
* **Git Audit Readiness:** Your repository must show incremental commits from all team members. "Big Bang" pushes of code right before the deadline will trigger an audit.
